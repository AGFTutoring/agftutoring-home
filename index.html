Course selection, essays, testing, interviews and positioning brought together as one process rather than a series of disconnected tasks. About Alastair Fisher The educator behind AGF Tutoring. My work over the past thirty years has centred on a single question: how students come to understand difficult ideas clearly, and how that clarity translates into performance under examination conditions. My academic background combines the sciences with a longstanding interest in language, cognition and the way people learn. That combination has shaped a method that is both analytical and diagnostic: I am interested not merely in what a student gets wrong, but in where understanding first begins to slip. Most academic difficulty does not arise from lack of ability. It arises from confusion left unresolved — often small at first, but increasingly costly under pressure. My work is to identify those points of divergence, rebuild understanding from first principles, and translate that understanding into precise, confident performance. Beyond the lesson The aim is not simply higher grades, though grades matter. It is the development of a more stable and exact way of thinking. That is why my work frequently extends beyond a single subject into academic mentoring, university choices, applications and longer-term educational strategy. The method is simple in outline but exacting in practice. The visible mistake is often only the last link in a much longer chain. 01 Diagnose Find where understanding first breaks down, not simply where the final answer goes wrong. 02 Rebuild Restore the concept from first principles, using intuition before procedure. 03 Clarify Introduce exact language, formal structure and disciplined written expression. 04 Test Use carefully chosen questions to establish whether the idea is genuinely secure. 05 Extend Move from competence to mastery, particularly with unfamiliar questions and pressure.
